# python-nursing-assistant-analysis-project
Hello, welcome to my Nursing Assistant Analysis Project. 

## What this project does:
My friend, who works as a nursing assistant, moved to West Virginia from California a few years ago. She often complains how they aren't treated fairly and have to do a lot of work. This made me wonder about how many Nursing Assistants are in her state and if that could contribute to it. 
In this project, I seek to answer the question: Which states have the highest density of nursing assistants? I use data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics to find this answer. I also analyze reasons why these states have a higher number of Nursing Assistant than national average.
## Why this project is useful: 
This information is useful because knowing the concentration of a job in one area can help to anticipate potential issue. For example, a state that has more nursing assistants than national average could face problems such as overcrowing, underpaying of staff, and more. It also can help nursing assistants and those seeking to work this position to make decisions regarding their employment. If the state they live in has a higher density of nursing assistants then the rest, this could move them to seek other employment, or to leverage that position. 

## Outcomes:
West Virginia is one of the only 2 states(NY) that has nursing assistant as the 2nd most common profession.
